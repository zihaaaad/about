About Me

I am a software engineer and technical educator based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I specialize in building robust full-stack web applications, native desktop utilities, and practical AI solutions that solve real operational challenges for teams and organizations.

Currently, I serve as a Trainer at the As-Sunnah Skill Development Institute (an initiative of the As-Sunnah Foundation), where I lead comprehensive, hands-on courses in Generative AI, digital literacy, and modern web development. Teaching is a central passion of mine—I love breaking down complex technical workflows into accessible, actionable engineering skills that empower learners to build real things.

When I am not in the classroom, I engineer production software across web, mobile, and desktop ecosystems. I care deeply about clean architecture, reliable offline data storage, and high-performance user interfaces.

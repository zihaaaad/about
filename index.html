About Me

Hello! I am Zihad Hasan, a software engineer and educator based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I specialize in building full-stack web platforms, native desktop utilities, and practical AI integrations that solve real operational challenges for teams and organizations.

Currently, I serve as a Trainer at the As-Sunnah Skill Development Institute (an initiative of the As-Sunnah Foundation), where I lead hands-on courses in Generative AI, digital literacy, and modern web development. Teaching is a core passion of mine—I love breaking down complex technical workflows into accessible, practical knowledge that empowers learners to build real things.

When I'm not in the classroom, I engineer production software across web, mobile, and desktop. Some of my recent open-source work includes Rupantor (a desktop font manager with Adobe After Effects integration), Chuti (an offline-first leave management suite), and EchoScript (a background audio recorder powered by Gemini AI). I care deeply about clean architecture, reliable local data storage, and high-performance user interfaces.
